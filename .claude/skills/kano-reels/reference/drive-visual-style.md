# Inspiration style report (29 PNGs: 9 text, 3 on-screen, 17 loose)

## 1. Caption / text style ("text inspo": 9 vertical 9:16 podcast-clip frames)
- **Type pairing is the signature:** a clean geometric/grotesk **sans** (white, light-to-medium weight, small) for setup words, with **one keyword set in a large condensed/italic serif or script** (Didone-style italic, e.g. "Resting Heart Rate", "Wind Down", "steal", "gap between", "30,000ft"). Some use a heavy all-caps sans for the keyword instead ("GROWTH", "Social media", "$100,000").
- **Size:** setup line is about 4–6% of frame width per cap height. The keyword runs 2–3x larger and spans about 50–70% of frame width.
- **Colours:** white for most words. The keyword is highlighted in **warm orange/amber** (#F5A623 to #E8892B, the most common), **gold/cream** (#F2D9A0), or **acid lime** (#C6F432) in one case. Only the colour changes; there's no box or pill behind the text.
- **Stroke/shadow:** no outline. There's a soft dark drop shadow or glow for legibility, and the footage is graded slightly dark and warm, so the text reads without a plate.
- **Placement:** centre-lower third, roughly 55–70% down the frame, often overlapping the speaker's chest or mic. It's centred, with tight leading and the lines stacked like a mini lockup.
- **Words per line:** 1–3 small words above (e.g. "What's the", "Lower Your"), then **1–2 hero words**, sometimes followed by a tiny 1–3 word tail ("view on", "Before Bed", "TODAY").
- **Name tags:** a speaker name in a serif or bold sans, with a tiny grey title underneath ("Bryan Johnson / Health and wellness expert"). It sits lower-left or mid-right, sometimes with a thin rule line.

## 2. On-screen / motion-graphic style (on-screen inspo + loose UI shots)
- **Backgrounds** are near-black (#05050A to #0D0D14), deep navy, or indigo. Depth comes from big soft **radial glows** behind the subject, plus faint dot grids, line grids or perspective floors. Edges vignette to black.
- **Cards:** rounded rectangles with a 16–28px-equivalent radius, or pill/capsule shapes for chips, search bars and stat toggles. Squircle app-icon tiles are common.
- **Glass and glow:** frosted-glass panels with a thin 1px light inner border (white at 10–20% opacity), subtle top-edge highlights and backdrop blur. Colour **rim-light / edge glow** bleeds from behind cards. Examples: orange-left/blue-right on an AI prompt bar, a neon bolt over a frosted card, light beams entering and leaving a card.
- **Icon tiles:** monochrome white line icons inside dark or glass squircles. Some are arranged around **concentric orbit rings** with small dots, others in a node to arrow to node flow.
- **Palettes:**
  - electric blue / indigo / violet (#3B5BFF, #6A4CFF, #8B5CF6)
  - magenta-pink accents (#E040FB)
  - one-off warm sets: orange gradient (#FF6A00), neon yellow (#F5E642), emerald green (#2BD67B)
  - multicolour rainbow "fibre" ribbons merging into a single capsule
- **Data viz:** big bold white numerals ("$63,921", "75.6%", "102 USDT") with tiny grey labels. Thin gradient progress rings, glowing area charts with a highlighted dot and a tooltip pill, and an upward curve with a glowing marker.
- **Layouts:** a single hero object centred with lots of negative space, or a horizontal row of 3 cards with the middle one emphasised (bigger and brighter while the side cards are dimmer). There are also staggered checklists that fade out toward the bottom, and flow diagrams.

## 3. Recurring motifs worth copying for a 9:16 marketing reel
1. **Sans + italic-serif keyword caption** in white with one orange or gold hero word, placed centre-lower. Animate the hero word with a scale-up and fade (about 1.1 to 1.0) while the small words pop in per word.
2. **Darken or blur the talking-head footage** (40–60% black overlay plus blur) when a UI card appears. Keep the card centred with a coloured radial glow behind it.
3. **Frosted glass cards with a 1px light border and coloured rim glow.** Use one accent hue per scene (blue/violet as the brand default, with warm orange for "growth/money" beats).
4. **Big-number stat beats:** count-up numerals, a gradient ring filling, or a glowing line chart drawing on.
5. **Icon squircles orbiting a glowing core**, or a node flow (A to B to C) with light streaks travelling along the connectors.
6. **Pill UI elements** such as a search bar ("X vs Y"), CTA buttons ("Generate"), checklists and toggles. They're quick to animate and work well as hooks or CTAs.
7. **Vignette, dot or line grid backgrounds plus light beams.** Keep them dark and high-contrast, put the glow behind the subject, and leave generous safe margins for 9:16.
